Hi,

My name is Luca Nobili D’Avach, and I explore all the possible ways to express and build brand identities.

I work across graphic design, video editing, event production, illustration, and online and offline presence. To me these aren’t separate jobs, but different pieces of the same puzzle. I start from an idea and carry it everywhere it can exist.

Graduated in Business Administration at Roma Tre, and later in Luxury Management at LUISS. Late-night school: YouTube tutorials, Reddit, Discord, online talks.

As a teenager I grew up on hip-hop culture and sneaker Twitter, where I first understood the meaning of communities and brand worlds. Later the world of electronic music was introduced to me, and I started telling stories with sound, light and people.

Now I’m looking for the next room to create in.
